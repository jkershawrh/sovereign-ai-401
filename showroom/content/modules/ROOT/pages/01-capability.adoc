= 1. Observe capability

Inspect the cluster, node, and runtime configuration. Record whether Intel TDX,
the DCAP quote service, OpenShift Sandboxed Containers, and `kata-cc` are
present.

Capability is not workload proof. A CPU flag or `runtimeClassName` cannot show
that this pod launched as a confidential guest, produced a valid quote, or
matches approved measurements. Record unavailable observations as **UNKNOWN**
and keep the exercise in **REHEARSAL**.

Compare the source lab's known blocker: the TDX VM can launch, but the kata
agent does not complete its vsock connection. Explain why host capability is
still useful evidence—and why it cannot authorize a protected resource.
