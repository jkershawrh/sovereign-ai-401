= 3. Collect attestation evidence

Trace where a real confidential workload would request an Intel TDX quote and
where the quote service sits outside the workload boundary. Inspect the
rehearsal fixture's provenance before using it.

The fixture is not a cryptographic quote and is always labeled **REHEARSAL**.
Change its source state to `LIVE` without changing its evidence kind. The
service must refuse the false-live claim before appraisal or resource policy.

Record the live gate: confidential guest running, challenge-bound quote,
signature verified, and workload identity observed.
