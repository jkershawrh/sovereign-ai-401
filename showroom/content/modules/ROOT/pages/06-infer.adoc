= 6. Keep inference authorization separate

A valid appraisal and resource-policy match do not authorize a caller, prompt,
model, or inference request. Inspect the response and verify:

* `keyMaterialReleased` is `false`;
* inference authorization is `false`;
* `modelInvoked` is `false`;
* no latency or performance claim is present;
* LLM authority is `NONE`.

Describe the additional caller, model, residency, and usage policies a real
inference path would require. Do not add them to the receipt unless the
destination can observe and verify them.
