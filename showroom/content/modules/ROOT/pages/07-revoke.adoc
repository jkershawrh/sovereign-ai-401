= 7. Revoke trust

Revoke the approved rehearsal measurement, then submit the previously allowed
workload with a fresh nonce. The service must refuse it with
`MEASUREMENT_REVOKED`.

This exercise proves that trust is evaluated at request time rather than
granted permanently. Record the reference-values version, revocation actor,
timestamp, and the denied follow-up receipt.

Only named humans may change reference values. The lab does not promote policy
or repair the confidential runtime automatically.
