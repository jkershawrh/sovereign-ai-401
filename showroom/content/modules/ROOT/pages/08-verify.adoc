= 8. Verify evidence and reclaim

Verify that capability, challenge, evidence, appraisal, authorization, refusal,
and revocation records share one correlation identifier and valid digest links.
The complete factory journey proves deterministic **REHEARSAL** behavior only.

It does not prove a running confidential guest, a current hardware-rooted TDX
quote, protected-resource delivery, confidential inference, or performance.
Launchpad must independently qualify those facts in the destination cluster.

Uninstall the participant-scoped release and verify that pods, routes, services,
policies, temporary evidence, and credentials leave zero residue. Record any
cluster-owned shared operators separately; participants do not remove them.
