= Operate confidential AI with Intel TDX

This 75–90 minute lab uses **REHEARSAL** fixtures until the destination proves a running confidential guest and a fresh Intel TDX quote. It does not release a secret, invoke a model, or claim latency or performance.

You will operate the complete trust lifecycle:

CAPABILITY → CHALLENGE → ATTEST → APPRAISE → AUTHORIZE → INFER → REVOKE → VERIFY

Each exercise separates observed evidence from policy and authority. The
deterministic service may issue a synthetic receipt, but only a named human can
approve deployment, reference-value changes, certification, or promotion.
